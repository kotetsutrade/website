Featured Platform — Hydrotalcite (LDH)

Flame-retardant performance, engineered at the nanoscale

Our premium Hydrotalcite (Layered Double Hydroxide) platform is built from 29 nm nanoplatelets with an exceptionally narrow particle-size distribution (span 0.9) — enabling refractive-index matching for optical transparency even at low filler loading, alongside a three-tier synergistic flame-retardant architecture.

  • Endothermic barrier (LDH) + intumescent phosphorus chemistry (APP) + smoke-suppression synergist (ZnO)
  • Halogen-free, antimony-free — compliant with modern eco-regulatory expectations
  • Low heavy-metal content, ICP-verified for consistent purity
  • Optical clarity retained at low filler loading via refractive-index matching

Manufacturing Philosophy

Precision at the process level, not just the recipe level

Performance advantages are built into how our partner manufacturers make each material — long before any formulation know-how is applied.

Continuous-Flow Reactor Production

Rather than batch kettles, our sourced materials are produced in continuous-flow reactor systems that hold residence time, temperature, and reagent dosing constant — minimizing batch-to-batch deviation and enabling precise, repeatable particle-size control at industrial scale.

Room-Temperature, Aqueous Synthesis

Crystallization is carried out at ambient temperature in water-based systems — eliminating organic solvents, reducing energy input, and delivering a genuinely lower-carbon-footprint production pathway without compromising crystal quality.

Water Dispersion Technology

Why our aqueous dispersions outperform dry-powder redispersion

Many of our platforms are available as stabilized water-based dispersions — engineered for formulators who need nano-scale performance without the handling burden of dry nanopowders.

Permanent De-Agglomeration

Proprietary dispersant chemistry locks primary nanoparticles apart electrostatically and sterically, preventing the re-agglomeration that plagues re-dispersed dry powders.

Zero Dust Exposure

Liquid handling eliminates nanoparticle airborne dust — improving worker safety and occupational hygiene on the customer's production floor.

VOC-Free, Solvent-Free

Water as the carrier medium means no flash-point risk, no solvent recovery cost, and direct compatibility with waterborne coatings, adhesives, and textile finishes.

High Solids, Low Viscosity

Controlled particle-size distribution allows high active-content loading while maintaining pumpable, low-viscosity behavior for line-speed compatible processing.

Optical Clarity Retained

True nanoscale dispersion (not aggregates) preserves transparency in films, coatings, and cosmetic bases where haze is unacceptable.

Extended Shelf Stability

Controlled pH and ionic strength deliver long-term sedimentation resistance, so dispersions remain ready-to-use after extended storage.

Eliminates a Processing Step

Customers skip energy-intensive milling and dry redispersion entirely — reducing capex and cycle time on their own production line.

Lower Environmental Footprint

Room-temperature aqueous processing aligns with green-chemistry sourcing requirements increasingly demanded by global brand owners.
