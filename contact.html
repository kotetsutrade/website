Frequently Asked

Before you reach out

Do you sell small trial quantities?

Yes — we support trial and pilot-scale quantities for formulation validation before committing to full production volumes. Mention your target trial size in your inquiry.

Can materials be shipped internationally?

Yes — from our Bangkok base with Eastern Seaboard port access, we regularly ship across ASEAN and can discuss broader export logistics on request.

Do you offer true private-label packaging?

Yes — many of our customers sell finished compounds under their own brand. We support custom labeling and documentation to match your specifications.

Can you share technical data before purchase?

Absolutely — particle-size distribution curves, ICP results, and application-relevant test data are available under a standard technical discussion with our team.
