Our Story

Why we exist

The specialty chemicals market is crowded with commodity traders reselling the same generic-grade powders under a dozen different labels. Meanwhile, some of the most technically capable producers of high-performance inorganic materials — precision nanoplatelet hydrotalcite, narrow-distribution nano-ZnO, ultra-pure alumina hydrates — remain invisible to the formulators who need them most, because they lack international distribution reach.

Kotetsu Trade Co., Ltd. was founded in Bangkok to close that gap. We identify these producers, qualify their materials rigorously, and bring them to market under private-label programs — paired with our own proprietary compounding know-how to unlock performance that neither the raw base material nor a naive physical blend could achieve alone.

How We Work

From base material to finished formulation

Every private-label compound in our portfolio passes through a disciplined four-stage development and quality process.

01

Global Sourcing & Qualification

We scout specialty manufacturers worldwide and rigorously test particle morphology, purity, and consistency before any material enters our portfolio.

02

Proprietary Formulation

Our technical team applies advanced compounding know-how — surface treatment, synergist ratios, dispersion chemistry — to elevate the base material.

03

Analytical Verification

Particle size distribution, ICP heavy-metal screening, and application-specific performance testing validate every production lot.

04

Private-Label Delivery

Materials ship under your specification and label, backed by our technical documentation and ongoing formulation support.

Our Journey

Milestones

FOUNDATION

Establishment in Bangkok

Kotetsu Trade Co., Ltd. is founded as a specialty chemical trading house, focused on connecting Thailand's polymer, coatings, and cosmetics industries with hard-to-find, high-performance inorganic materials.

PORTFOLIO EXPANSION

Flame-Retardant Platform Development

Development of our three-tier synergistic flame-retardant architecture combining hydrotalcite (LDH), ammonium polyphosphate (APP), and zinc oxide (ZnO) smoke suppression.

TECHNICAL DEEPENING

Nano-Morphology Engineering

Introduction of multiple engineered ZnO morphologies — spheres, flakes, stars, and snowball structures — each tuned for a distinct application performance profile.

TODAY
